Eight modules, one core

01

Human Resource

One employee record drives attendance, payroll and safety training.

What is covered

  • Staff hiring: welders, electricians, panel wiring technicians, engineers
  • Attendance, salary, training, safety compliance
  • Monthly reporting of production, sales, profit, expenses

02

Quotations and Customers

Quote to order in one click, with the price list always current.

What is covered

  • Quotations, orders and follow-ups for contractors, builders, industries
  • Price list management: MS boxes, DBs, panels, switchgear
  • After-sales service and warranty tracking

03

Workshop Control

Every job order carries its BOM, stage and due date.

What is covered

  • Job order planning: cutting, bending, welding, powder coating, assembly, wiring
  • Daily production target and machine/workshop load planning
  • BOM for each panel and box type; coordination with coating and testing

04

Purchasing

Shortages from the BOM turn into purchase orders quickly.

What is covered

  • MS sheets, copper bus bars, breakers (ABB, Siemens, Schneider), wiring, powder, hardware
  • Vendor selection, rate comparison, PO issuance
  • Import and local purchase tracking, lead time management

05

Warehouse

Stock moves only with a slip, so counts stay accurate.

What is covered

  • Raw stock: MS sheets, profiles, copper, breakers
  • Finished goods: MS boxes, panels, switchgear
  • Issue to production by Gate Pass / Material Requisition Slip
  • Stock-in, stock-out, minimum stock level alerts

06

Quality Control

A panel cannot ship until its test report is signed off.

What is covered

  • Very important for switchgear: incoming and in-process inspection
  • Final testing: insulation, continuity, breaker tripping, powder coating thickness
  • Test reports maintained per client specification

07

Finance and Accounts

Real cost and profit for every product and order.

What is covered

  • Costing per product: material + labor + overhead
  • Invoicing, payments to vendors, receipts from customers
  • Payroll, expenses, profit/loss, GST / tax records

08

Design and Engineering

Drawings follow the order from quote to workshop.

What is covered

  • Panel drawings and Single Line Diagrams (SLD)
  • AutoCAD file management
  • Attachment with project or customer orders
